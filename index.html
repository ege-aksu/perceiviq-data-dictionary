Interactive field catalog for monthly and weekly SFTP deliverables plus flat reference files. Parquet / Snappy. Scope: US-headquartered companies from Revelio shared public & private lists; dynamics, postings, and sentiment time series start May 2021 where applicable. Sentiment scores: per-month confidence-weighted v2 NLP themes (sentiment_{theme}) at review location geo (region/country/state/metro/county), not HQ. Review trends: Glassdoor star ratings with min-cell privacy (num_reviews < 10 → undisclosed) and geography_grain in {county, state, region}. Cert/course aggregates use the same privacy + geography_grain pattern; coverage companions report match rates. Paths are relative to the PerceivIQ SFTP root: <YYYYMM> identifies a monthly batch and <YYYYMMDD> identifies a Friday weekly delivery. Individual reviews / certs / courses SFTP unloads are currently paused.
